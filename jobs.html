1. EdTech Web Developer

Ref Number: ED101

Description: Develop and optimize interactive e-learning web applications, ensuring seamless accessibility and performance for students and educators.

Salary: $100,000 - $120,000 / year

Reports to: Lead Engineering Manager

Key Responsibilities

  1. Build responsive frontend components for our virtual classroom dashboard.
  2. Ensure web accessibility compliance (WCAG standards) for diverse learners.
  3. Collaborate with UI/UX designers to integrate seamless student learning tools.

Requirements

  • Essential: Strong proficiency in HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript (ES6+).
  • Essential: Experience building mobile-responsive educational or SaaS web interfaces.
  • Preferable: Familiarity with React.js and web accessibility guidelines.

2. EdTech UI/UX Designer

Ref Number: UX025

Description: Design intuitive, user-friendly interfaces for our online learning platform, focusing on enhancing engagement for students of all ages.

Salary: $90,000 - $110,000 / year

Reports to: Head of Product Design

Key Responsibilities

  1. Conduct user research with students and teachers to improve learning experiences.
  2. Create wireframes, high-fidelity prototypes, and design systems in Figma.
  3. Test interface usability to ensure inclusive designs for users with disabilities.

Requirements

  • Essential: Proven experience designing web or mobile educational apps.
  • Essential: Proficiency with prototyping tools such as Figma or Adobe XD.
  • Preferable: Basic understanding of front-end styling (HTML/CSS).
